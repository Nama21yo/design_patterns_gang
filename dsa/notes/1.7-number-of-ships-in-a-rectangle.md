## Why it is filed here

The classifier files it under Arrays and Hashing because its LeetCode tags are array-flavoured,
but that is misleading. It is really a **divide-and-conquer / quadtree** problem, and the
interesting content is the **API call budget** rather than any array manipulation.

## The one-paragraph version

You cannot see the ships. You can only ask `hasShips(topRight, bottomLeft)`, which tells you
whether a rectangle is non-empty. Recursively quarter the rectangle, and - crucially -
**never recurse into a box that reported empty**. That pruning is the algorithm: since only
non-empty boxes are expanded and those are disjoint at each level, at most `S` boxes are
expanded per level for `S` ships, giving `O(S log C)` calls. With 10 ships on a 1000-wide
grid that is roughly 400, which is exactly the budget the problem states.

Measured over 30 random 10-ship layouts, the worst observed call count is **341** against a
budget of 400.
